KTH - Kitchen Table Highlander (a ‘Commander’ variant)

(Drafted by jpxsat on 2026-08-02 /Status: Alpha)

 

A - Construction:

·      You choose your avatar, a creature that sits at the helm of your deck, the embodiment of your power - this is a legendary creature, uncommon at most. This defines the “identity” of your deck color(s).

·      99-cards deck

·      11-card sideboard (this is mostly targeted at Lessons / Learn strategies)

·      Total cards : 111

 

B - Similarities with commander:

·      A single character is at the helm of your deck (your Avatar)

·      In practical terms, every “commander rule” applies, but with some differences…

 

C - Key differences against regular commander:

·      Your Avatar is not technically your Commander, so:

o   ❌ No commander damage

o   ❌ No “Commander-related” cards (e.g. Command Tower, Arcane Signet, etc.)

·      The Avatar “lives” in the sideboard instead of a dedicated area. Every practical rule about the command zone referring to the commander, is changed to the sideboard.

·      20 lives - the way the game was originally designed!

·      Defined maximums: idea? library, Mana base, Hand size, everything should be limited

o   Max life total: 40 - after that, you don’t gain life if your life total is already at its max.

o   Max counters/tokens: tokens & counters on cards are capped at 6 (for ease of using 6 sided dices).

§  This is intended to cap infinite loops and to keep sanity on the board.

§  After that, tokens are not created

§  After that, counters are not put on the cards.

 

D - Card restrictions:

·      ❌ Any cards from other known formats special lists (e.g. Reserved list, Commander “Game Changers”, Canadian Highlander points list — which includes cards like Sol Ring)

·      ❌ Any card that mentions or references “Commander”

·      ❌ Maximum rarity: Uncommon (was it printed as an uncommon or common at some point? Checks!)

·      This is intended to be around Bracket 2 power level (point being: avoid comboing with 2 cards and stuff like that 🤯)

 

E - Ban list? Yes! 😄

·      No need to reinvent the wheel — this format inherits all banned cards from other Official & Popular Magic formats.

o   They’re banned because they became nasty somehow, right?

·      The word “search” is banned (as a mechanic, not as card name) - (reduces turn time by avoiding decision paralysis and time shuffling - and increases game-to-game variance — embrace the chaos!! 😄)

o   This kills the initiative as one of the rooms makes you search a land

 

F - Design philosophy / Idea :

·      To feel “old-school and simple” Magic (lower rarity normally implies simpler cards, hence simpler games)

·      Pack some more « Oomf » than Pauper Commander, so you can actually finish the games in style.

·      Having a format that grows slow by having a limited amount of available creatures to put at the helm of your deck.

·      The banned cards is a way to try to avoid ubiquitous cards (ex: Swords to plowshares is in every deck where it can be used - we try to avoid this in order for each deck to feel flavourful)

·      Create a naturally low-power, non-broken, fair environment without being boring.

·      Take away “too high advantaged cards”. Just imagine turn 1 Command Tower, Sol Ring, Arcane Signet…

o   I mean… come on… Command Tower enters untapped, and gives you every color you need at no cost? In contrast, the equivalent in this format comes to be City of Brass - yes, it gives you any color but pains you every time it becomes tapped!

·      Avoid free/unfair spells

·      Encourage:

o   More turns per game but faster turns (expected: around 10 turns to win)

o   Games with high interaction between players (less solitaire games)

o   Few board wipes (but still some, as opposed to Pauper Commander where they’re almost non-existent)

o   A balanced, fair, and cheap mana base (most nonbasic lands enter tapped in this format)

o   Use of cheap, forgotten and/or underplayed cards that pack tasteful power

·      Paced games that rely more on the ability of the player - not on the price you payed for the card (there’s of course some expensive cards… but hopefully all the boundaries are taking out brutally expensive cards)

·      Highly synergic and flavorful decks are expected

o   Most likely we will see a lot of combos built on +3 cards

·      Tutors are naturally excluded

o   This will make the combos a challenge to build

·      The rules themselves should prevent broken play, without constant intervention (as the ban list relies on other formats)
